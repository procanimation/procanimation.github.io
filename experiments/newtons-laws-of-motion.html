In 1687, Isaac Newton unlocked the true architecture of the physical universe with three elegant laws. Forces do not create motion; forces create acceleration. The Three Foundations of Classical Reality

1st Law: The Law of Inertia

$$\sum \mathbf{F} = 0 \implies \mathbf{v} = \text{constant}$$. An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion continues with constant velocity in a straight line unless acted upon by a net force.

2nd Law: The Accelerator Pedal

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{net}} = m \mathbf{a} \implies \mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{F}}{m}$$. Net force causes acceleration (rate of change of velocity). Doubling mass cuts acceleration in half for the same force.

3rd Law: Action & Reaction

$$\mathbf{F}_{AB} = -\mathbf{F}_{BA}$$. Forces never exist alone; they are equal and opposite interactions between two bodies. The lighter body accelerates faster, but the Center of Mass never budges.
