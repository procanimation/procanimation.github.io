In 1687, Sir Isaac Newton published the Principia, completely solving the two-body gravitational problem: two masses orbiting under an inverse-square force trace exact, eternal conic sections (circles, ellipses, parabolas, or hyperbolas). But when Newton attempted to calculate the simultaneous gravitational interactions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon, he confessed that "the three-body problem made his head ache and kept him awake so often that he would think of it no more."

The Celestial Mechanics Lexicon

Poincaré Non-Integrability

In 1887, Henri Poincaré proved that the general three-body problem cannot be integrated in terms of algebraic functions, birthing modern chaos theory.

Lagrange Equilibrium Points

Joseph-Louis Lagrange discovered five equilibrium positions (L1–L5) where centrifugal and gravitational forces cancel out.

Figure-Eight Choreography

In 1993, Chenciner and Montgomery discovered a zero-angular-momentum stable choreography where 3 equal masses chase each other along a figure-8.

Symplectic Integration

Numerical schemes (like Verlet and Runge-Kutta) that conserve total energy $E = T + V$ without artificial orbital decay over long times.

1. The Governing Differential Equations

For $N = 3$ point masses $m_1, m_2, m_3$ with position vectors $\mathbf{r}_1, \mathbf{r}_2, \mathbf{r}_3 \in \mathbb{R}^3$, Newton’s universal gravitation dictates:

Newton's Gravitational System
$$m_i \frac{d^2\mathbf{r}_i}{dt^2} = \sum_{j \neq i}^3 G \frac{m_i m_j}{|\mathbf{r}_j - \mathbf{r}_i|^3} (\mathbf{r}_j - \mathbf{r}_i)$$

This represents a set of 18 coupled first-order nonlinear differential equations. Standard conservation laws provide 10 integrals of motion:

  • Conservation of Linear Momentum (3 integrals): $\sum m_i \dot{\mathbf{r}}_i = \mathbf{P}_{\text{total}}$
  • Conservation of Center of Mass (3 integrals): $\sum m_i \mathbf{r}_i = \mathbf{P}_{\text{total}} t + \mathbf{R}_0$
  • Conservation of Angular Momentum (3 integrals): $\mathbf{L} = \sum m_i (\mathbf{r}_i \times \dot{\mathbf{r}}_i)$
  • Conservation of Total Energy (1 integral): $E = \frac{1}{2}\sum m_i |\dot{\mathbf{r}}_i|^2 - \sum_{i < j} \frac{G m_i m_j}{|\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j|}$

Reducing the 18 degrees of freedom by these 10 constants leaves 8 unsolved dimensions. Bruns (1887) and Poincaré (1889) proved that no additional independent algebraic integrals of motion exist. The system is analytically non-integrable.

// Procanimation World — 3-Body Gravitational Acceleration Computation
function computeGravitationalAccelerations(bodies, G, softening) {
  const accels = bodies.map(() => ({ ax: 0, ay: 0 }));

  for (let i = 0; i < bodies.length; i++) {
    for (let j = i + 1; j < bodies.length; j++) {
      const dx = bodies[j].x - bodies[i].x;
      const dy = bodies[j].y - bodies[i].y;
      const distSq = dx * dx + dy * dy + softening * softening;
      const dist = Math.sqrt(distSq);
      const forceMag = (G / (distSq * dist));

      // Force on body i due to j
      accels[i].ax += forceMag * bodies[j].m * dx;
      accels[i].ay += forceMag * bodies[j].m * dy;

      // Equal and opposite reaction on body j
      accels[j].ax -= forceMag * bodies[i].m * dx;
      accels[j].ay -= forceMag * bodies[i].m * dy;
    }
  }
  return accels;
}
