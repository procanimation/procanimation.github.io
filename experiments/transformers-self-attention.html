For decades, natural language processing was trapped in sequential assembly lines. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and LSTMs forced neural networks to step word by word, bottlenecking hardware across time and suffering from catastrophic forgetting over long documents. In 2017, the seminal paper "Attention Is All You Need" shattered this paradigm by introducing the Transformer. By replacing recurrence entirely with Self-Attention, modern AI can process entire libraries in parallel, dynamically routing context between distant concepts with mathematical precision.

The Foundations of Attention & Transformers

1. The Recurrent Bottleneck

$$h_t = \tanh(W_{hh} h_{t-1} + W_{xh} x_t)$$. Sequential recurrence prohibits GPU parallelization because step $$t$$ strictly depends on $$t-1$$. Repeated matrix multiplications cause gradients $$\frac{\partial h_T}{\partial h_1} \propto (W_{hh}^T)^T \to 0$$ to vanish exponentially, severing long-range document memory.

2. The Query, Key & Value Trinity

$$\mathbf{Q} = \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}^Q, \; \mathbf{K} = \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}^K, \; \mathbf{V} = \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}^V$$. Inspired by database search, each token is projected into three distinct vectors: a Query (what am I looking for?), a Key (what identity/content do I offer?), and a Value (what semantic information do I communicate if matched?).

3. Scaled Dot-Product Attention

$$\text{Attention}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V}) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)\mathbf{V}$$. Because the variance of vector dot products scales proportionally with dimension $$d_k$$, large dimensions push Softmax into flat saturated tails with zero gradients. Dividing by $$\sqrt{d_k}$$ stabilizes variance to 1.0, enabling stable gradient backpropagation.

4. Multi-Head Specialization

$$\text{MultiHead} = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \dots, \text{head}_h)\mathbf{W}^O$$. Language is multi-faceted. Projecting into $$h$$ parallel lower-dimensional subspaces allows separate attention heads to independently specialize in grammatical syntax, pronoun co-reference, and distant causal relationships simultaneously.
