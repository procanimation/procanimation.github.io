Traditional 2D digital animation relies on pre-rendered sprite sheets or skeletal puppet deformers with rigid polygon meshes. In this project, we engineer a pure code-driven procedural cartoon studio in HTML5 Canvas. Combining continuous Bezier organic limbs (eliminating unsightly puppet knee and elbow seams), hierarchical coordinate trees, harmonic secondary micro-springs, and real-time FFT formant extraction for acoustic lip-sync, we bring a four-member cartoon family to life across four distinct architectural and natural settings in the edutainment story: "Why Does the Sky Change Color?"

Procedural Animation & Rigging Lexicon

Hierarchical Forward Kinematics (FK)

Every joint down the anatomical skeleton inherits position and rotation from its parent via coordinate stack transforms: $$\mathbf{T}_{\text{hand}} = \mathbf{T}_{\text{root}} \cdot \mathbf{T}_{\text{spine}} \cdot \mathbf{T}_{\text{shoulder}} \cdot \mathbf{R}(\theta_1) \cdot \mathbf{T}_{\text{elbow}} \cdot \mathbf{R}(\theta_2)$$ Upper arms dictate elbow pivots, which in turn govern forearm rotation, wrist orientation, and hand gesture poses without joint dislocations.

Continuous Bezier Limbs vs Puppet Seams

Segmented cutout puppets suffer from harsh circular seams at knees and elbows. We resolve this by drawing limbs as continuous, tapering cubic Bezier splines whose anchor points are bounded by smooth semi-circular caps: $$\mathbf{B}(t) = (1-t)^3 \mathbf{P}_0 + 3(1-t)^2 t \mathbf{P}_1 + 3(1-t)t^2 \mathbf{P}_2 + t^3 \mathbf{P}_3$$ This yields organic muscle curvature and natural fabric creases when joints flex.

Acoustic Formant Extraction & Lip-Sync

Rather than guessing mouth shapes from arbitrary timers, the speech engine samples Web Audio FFT frequency bins in real time: $$\text{F1 (180–750 Hz)} \implies \text{Jaw Drop (Vowels)}, \quad \text{F2 (800–2000 Hz)} \implies \text{Tongue Resonance (/i/, /e/)}$$ Consonant dips instantly compress the lips into closed bilabial curves (/m/, /b/, /p/), synchronized to within a single frame (16.6ms) of voice audio.

Grounded Breathing & Saccadic Gaze

To eliminate distracting "floaty" vertical bouncing, the character root is firmly anchored to the floor ($y = \text{groundY}$). Subtle breathing dilates only the upper chest cavity by $\pm 1.2\%$. Listening characters smoothly glide their gaze toward the active speaker using damped exponential interpolation, while speakers glance warmly toward the camera.

Rayleigh Scattering Physics (Act 3)

In Act 3, Mark (Dad) demonstrates Lord Rayleigh's 1871 scattering law for particles much smaller than light wavelengths: $$I(\lambda) = I_0 \frac{1 + \cos^2 \theta}{2 R^2} \left(\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\right)^4 \left(\frac{n^2 - 1}{n^2 + 2}\right)^2 \left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^6 \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$$ Because 400nm blue light scatters $\approx 9.4\times$ more intensely than 700nm red light, high-frequency blue photons saturate the sky dome while red waves penetrate straight through.
