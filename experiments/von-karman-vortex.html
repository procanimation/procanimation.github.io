The Fluid Dynamics Lexicon

Reynolds Number ($Re$)

The dimensionless ratio of inertial forces to viscous forces: $Re = \frac{\rho U D}{\mu}$. At $Re < 40$, flow is smooth and laminar. Between $47 < Re < 200$, steady periodic vortex shedding emerges.

Strouhal Number ($St$)

A dimensionless parameter describing oscillating flow mechanisms: $St = \frac{f D}{U} \approx 0.21$ for circular cylinders, linking shedding frequency directly to flow velocity.

Boundary Layer Separation

Viscous friction decelerates fluid near the obstacle surface. Under an adverse pressure gradient, the flow reverses direction, detaching from the wall into a free shear layer.

Aeroelastic Resonance & Flutter

Every detaching vortex creates an asymmetric pressure drop, imparting a fluctuating lift force. If shedding frequency matches the structure's mechanical eigenfrequency, catastrophic failure occurs.
