The Gravitational Radiation Lexicon

The Einstein Quadrupole Formula

Unlike electromagnetism (where dipole radiation dominates), mass conservation forbids gravitational dipole radiation. The power radiated in gravitational waves depends on the third time derivative of the mass quadrupole moment: $P = -\frac{32}{5}\frac{G^4}{c^5}\frac{m_1^2 m_2^2(m_1 + m_2)}{r^5}$.

The Gravitational Strain ($h$)

A dimensionless measure of spacetime stretching and squeezing: $h = \frac{\Delta L}{L}$. When ripples from a billion light-years away reach Earth, the strain is $h \approx 10^{-21}$, changing a 4-kilometer laser arm by $\Delta L \approx 4 \times 10^{-18}\,\text{m}$ (less than $1/1000\text{th}$ of a proton diameter).

The Chirp Waveform

As gravitational radiation bleeds orbital energy away, the black holes draw closer, causing orbital velocity and frequency to accelerate dramatically: $f_{\text{GW}}(t) \propto (t_{\text{merger}} - t)^{-3/8}$. The resulting audio crescendo sweeps upwards in pitch and volume into the signature "Chirp."

Multi-Messenger Astronomy

In 2017, the GW170817 neutron star merger was observed simultaneously in gravitational waves and across the electromagnetic spectrum (gamma, X-ray, optical, radio). It confirmed that gravity travels at the speed of light ($|v_{\text{GW}} - c|/c < 10^{-15}$) and discovered the origin of the universe's gold and platinum.

The Geometry of Metric Strain

When a gravitational wave passes perpendicular to a ring of test particles, it does not exert a traditional pulling force. Instead, it alters the distance metric between them. Under the "plus" polarization ($h_+$), the space stretches along the vertical axis while compressing along the horizontal axis, before alternating half a cycle later:

$$h_{ij}(t) = \begin{pmatrix} h_+ & h_\times & 0 \\ h_\times & -h_+ & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \cos(\omega t - kz)$$

LIGO exploits this quadrupole symmetry. When the X-arm of the 4-kilometer interferometer stretches by $\Delta L$, the perpendicular Y-arm compresses by $-\Delta L$. The differential change in path length alters the optical interference condition, turning destructive dark fringes into measurable laser pulses.
